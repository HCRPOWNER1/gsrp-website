Play fair

Community standards

These are website starter rules. Replace or expand them to match your official GSRP Discord rules and department SOPs.

01 · Respect everyone

No harassment, slurs, threats, or targeted disrespect. Follow staff directions and keep disagreements civil.

02 · Roleplay realistically

Value the scene, avoid random disruption, and give others a fair chance to participate.

03 · No exploiting or cheating

Do not use exploits, unauthorized tools, or glitches to gain an unfair advantage.

04 · Follow staff instructions

If a scene is paused or staff need to resolve an issue, cooperate and use the proper report process.

05 · Keep scenes fair

Avoid random deathmatching, unrealistic driving, powergaming, and intentionally ruining active scenes.

06 · Protect private information

Never share another member's private information. Use official channels for reports and applications.

The official Discord rules and staff decisions take priority over this sample summary.
